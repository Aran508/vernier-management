# Master Prompt v3 – Paintshop 2 Work Station Layout (START-style arrows on every point)

Create a clean, professional top-view (plan view) shop-floor layout diagram for a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) document, in the style of an industrial-engineering station layout drawing. Title at top-left: "PAINTSHOP 2 — WORK STATION LAYOUT", subtitle "Plan view (top) · Not to scale · Material flow: Right → Left".

LAYOUT
1. Conveyor
   - Horizontal rectangular conveyor bed in the centre of the page, light grey, with end drums at both ends.
   - Two pairs of parallel chain rails running left to right (one pair near the top edge, one pair near the bottom edge).
   - Material flow RIGHT to LEFT, shown with small red arrows on the conveyor.

2. Hydraulic cylinders on the conveyor
   - 5 cylinders lying flat, side by side with equal spacing, oriented vertically (perpendicular to flow), resting across both rail pairs.
   - Rod-end clevis at the top end, cap-end mounting at the bottom end, steel-blue barrel.
   - Labelled C1 to C5 from right to left (in white circles on the barrels).

3. Operator sides
   - Top side of the conveyor is labelled "LHS"; bottom side is labelled "RHS".
   - Operators are green squares with a simple person icon, labelled M1–M7. Place each M-label to the LEFT of its square (not above/below), so arrows can reach the square's top/bottom edge.

4. Stages (4 vertical dashed zones across the conveyor, each with a dark-navy header tab carrying a white numbered circle and the stage name; zones ordered right → left; alternating white / very light blue tint)
   - Stage 1 – UNLOADING (rightmost, over C1): M1 on LHS only.
   - Stage 2 – DEFECT RECTIFICATION (over C2): M2 on LHS, M3 on RHS.
   - Stage 3 – OTHER ACCESSORY FITTING (over C3 and C4): M4 on LHS, M5 on RHS.
   - Stage 4 – COVERING & PALLETING (leftmost, over C5): M6 on LHS, M7 on RHS.

5. START-style arrows for EVERY point (all drawn from the right-hand margin, so each stage is entered like the start point)
   - Point 1: a bold, thick (4 px) horizontal arrow from a large circled "1" at the right margin, pointing left into M1. Label under it: "START – UNLOADING".
   - Points 2, 3, 4: a thinner (2.5 px) long arrow from a small circled number at the right margin, running left along its own horizontal lane and then turning 90° to point onto the operator box. The stage name sits just above each lane, right-aligned next to the circle.
     - LHS lanes run ABOVE the top operators, stacked 18 px apart (② nearest the operators, ④ farthest), pointing down onto M2, M4, M6.
     - RHS lanes run BELOW the bottom operators, stacked 18 px apart (② nearest, ④ farthest), pointing up onto M3, M5, M7.
     - Lines must never cross each other or cross another operator box.
   - Arrowheads: solid dark navy triangles.

6. Stage-wise manpower & work description strip along the bottom of the page (4 boxes, ordered right → left to match the flow, each with a dark-navy header, white numbered circle and stage name):
   - 1 UNLOADING – Manpower: 1 (M1) – "Demasking and unloading the cylinder from conveyor to flat tray."
   - 2 DEFECT RECTIFICATION – LHS: 1 (M2) · RHS: 1 (M3) – "Scuffing and rework of the defect marked / spot touch-up with portable paint spray."
   - 3 OTHER ACCESSORY FITTING – LHS: 1 (M4) · RHS: 1 (M5) – "Grease application on masked unpainted area, dust seal assembly / covering of rod eyes / CEC."
   - 4 COVERING & PALLETING – Manpower: 2 (M6, M7) – "After ensuring all assembly and quality inspection, cylinder to be unloaded and palletized."
   - Manpower line in green bold, description in dark-navy regular text.

DO NOT INCLUDE
- No work tables.
- No legend box.
- No title block / SOP document-control table.

STYLE
- 2D CAD-style technical drawing, white background, dark-navy (#1F2A44) line work, double-line page border.
- Clean sans-serif (Arial/Helvetica) labels, consistent line weights.
- Colours: conveyor light grey, cylinders steel blue, operators green, stage headers dark navy with white text, red conveyor-flow arrows.
- No perspective, no 3D, no clutter, no decorative background. Landscape A4 (297 × 210 mm), print-ready.

STYLE VARIATION (swap in for "another style")
- Replace the single navy palette with one accent colour per stage (e.g. 1 = teal, 2 = amber, 3 = purple, 4 = crimson) used for the stage header, its zone tint, its arrows/circles and its description box, so each point is colour-coded end-to-end. Keep operators green and cylinders steel blue.
